Zombinator

PyGame · playable on itch.io

A shooter/strategy zombie game I made in grade 10 using the PyGame Python module — now playable in the browser on itch.io.

Press Play to load the game. WASD move · mouse shoot · Q interact · P pause.

This is a rough-around-the-edges project I started and finished in the last week of my grade 10 winter break for a final. I learned a lot about object-oriented programming along the way.

Sprite animation, movement, and bullets were adapted from programarcadegames.com. Music: NCS — Chasing Ghosts.

Survive endless zombie waves as a space marine. Earn cash to buy suit upgrades and buildings (banks for passive income, walls for defense). Harder zombies — and tanks — spawn as waves progress.
